# Varientropy Hypothesis (Working Draft)

## H1: Divergence-Controlled Hypothesis Generation

### Claim
High-entropy generation plus a novelty filter can yield hypotheses
that are novel relative to a known corpus while staying falsifiable.

### Formula Signature
Div(h) = H(h) - I(h; D_known)

### Falsification Test
If Div(h) scores are uncorrelated with expert novelty ratings on a
blind set, the claim is falsified.

### Uncertainty
Bootstrap intervals over 100 generations with different seeds.
